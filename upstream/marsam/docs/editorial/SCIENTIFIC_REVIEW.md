# Scientific review policy

MARSAM separates bibliographic identity, inspected scope, extraction, editorial synthesis, language review and scientific approval.

A public record may be source-checked while still remaining an AI-assisted draft. Human scientific approval is never inferred from a passing build.

High-risk claims about intervention effects, suicide or self-harm, diagnosis, cut scores, trauma, adverse religious experiences or individual clinical decisions require independent human scientific review before they can be labelled reviewed.

Study design must remain visible. Cross-sectional association, qualitative experience, psychometric evidence, longitudinal evidence, systematic review and randomized intervention evidence are not interchangeable.

Founder authorship does not increase inclusion priority. Relevance and marginal information value come first.
