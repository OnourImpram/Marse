# Corrections and post-publication updates

MARSAM should monitor DOI metadata, corrections, expressions of concern and retractions without silently overwriting editorial records.

A detected update enters review before changing a public claim. The editorial decision records the affected source, affected claims, reason, date and release.

Crossref or another metadata service is evidence of a bibliographic update, not automatic authority to rewrite scientific interpretation.

Retractions and material corrections invalidate dependent syntheses until their impact has been reviewed.
