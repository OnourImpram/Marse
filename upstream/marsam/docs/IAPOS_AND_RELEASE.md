## Authorization

The user authorized development and delivery of website source in their repository. This is not authorization to publish private research, claim center establishment, list an official board, collect participant data, create a clinical service, deploy to a public domain or incur paid service costs.

The design/spec files are proposals, not recorded approval from the future center. Academic content approval, translation approval, institutional statements and production release remain human decisions.

`canPublish` demonstrates a version-bound approval shape and fails on draft records. It is **not an authentication system**. The generator refuses `PUBLISH=true`; production generation is not implemented until governance, identity, rights and release decisions are recorded. Merely editing a metadata flag is not a real-world scientific authorization.

## Future release checklist

- Confirm legal/institutional status, name, official affiliation and authorized representatives.
- Approve each public identity statement and exact version of original content.
- Review all five language versions for equal evidential commitment, not just key parity.
- Review source identity, local support, currency and permission at the intended publication date.
- Confirm the rights to every downloadable or embedded asset. Keep tools link-only unless permission is recorded.
- Decide the real editorial backend, role-based access, backups and correction process.
- Complete live browser, mobile, assistive-technology, CSP and deployment-path checks.
- Review privacy notice, host access logs and any future analytics separately.
- Do not enable surveys before study-specific governance, consent and authorized data infrastructure exist.
- Approve an exact release manifest and deployment destination. Preview source delivery alone does not satisfy this step.

## Correction process

A proposed correction is recorded with source, affected content IDs, languages and reasoning. Changes must update the source ledger, translations and associated approval hashes. Technical rebuild and a new scientific/language review follow as appropriate. Previous review records are retained rather than overwritten to imply prior approval of changed text.
