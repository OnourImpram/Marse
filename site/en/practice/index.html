2023 · Research

A Spiritual Touch on Family Resilience: From Burnout to Hope

In this convergent parallel mixed-methods study, an eight-session (90 minutes each) spiritually oriented, positive psychology-based group counseling program on family resilience was implemented with ten mothers of primary school children; the one-group pretest–posttest comparison reported significant score increases in family resilience and use of spiritual resources, and thematic analysis identified six themes: hope, reliance (tawakkul), patience, social support, steps to change, and resilient attitude.
