Bidirectional associations between religiosity and mental health

Daniel Major-Smith, Jimmy Morgan, Isaac Halstead, Jean Golding

Preregistered analyses found mostly small or null-compatible bidirectional associations involving religiosity, depression and anxiety. Gender differences remained inconclusive. Residual confounding is possible in this observational design. Wide uncertainty and a historical parent cohort limit generalisation. Neither causal effects nor their absence are established.
