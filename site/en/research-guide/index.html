Start with what the study tests

Record the question, design, sample, country, setting and follow-up separately. Spirituality, religiosity, religious coping, integration preference and practice should not be entered as interchangeable constructs. Identify the instrument, respondent and measurement time for each variable.

Record findings with their limits

Record the comparator, main outcome, reported effect size and confidence interval. When only an abstract is available, do not infer missing methods. Do not label a cross-sectional association a causal effect. Examine selection bias, missing data, cultural context, funding and conflicts of interest separately.

Separate a study from a synthesis

A review additionally needs its scope, search date, information sources, eligibility criteria and selection decisions documented. PRISMA guides reporting, not a standalone quality score. The template here supports extraction. It is not a completed review or a risk-of-bias instrument.

Working files

Templates download empty. null means not yet extracted, not zero or not reported by the source. Field names use English for interoperability. Completed files are not sent to this site.
