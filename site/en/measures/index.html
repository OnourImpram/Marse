Evaluating measurement instruments

Consider construct, sample and conditions of use together. A scale score is not a diagnosis. Publication in one language does not validate other language versions.

Religious and Spiritual Struggles Scale (RSS-14)

The Turkish RSS-14 adaptation reported six dimensions in 495 Muslim participants. Total alpha was 0.82, subscales 0.60–0.82. These results do not establish invariance across worldviews or clinical cut-offs.

SWBS · Ekşi & Kardaş (2017) · 29

The 2017 development study reports 29 items, five response categories and three dimensions, transcendence, harmony with nature and anomie. Overall internal consistency is reported as .886. This instrument is not a Turkish translation of the 20-item Paloutzian and Ellison SWBS.

SWBS · Paloutzian & Ellison (1982) · 20

Twenty items use six response categories to assess religious and existential wellbeing. Each subscale has ten items. The religious domain includes a relationship with God. This is a different instrument from the 29-item Ekşi and Kardaş scale.

This study adapts the Decision to Forgive Scale (Davis et al., 2015) to Turkish culture: confirmatory factor analysis with 297 participants supported a one-dimensional structure, Cronbach's alpha was .91, and scores correlated significantly and positively with the Forgiveness of Others subscale of the Heartland Forgiveness Scale (r = .527).

Faith and Pornography: Moral Disapproval of Pornography Scale

This study develops a scale of moral disapproval of pornography, i.e. the psychological strain that arises when one's pornography use conflicts with one's moral beliefs, adapted to Turkish conditions so that people of differing religious beliefs and practices can answer it; exploratory factor analysis with 634 participants yielded a single factor explaining 71% of the total variance, confirmatory factor analysis in a second sample of 580 showed acceptable fit, and Cronbach's alpha was .862.

Spiritual Well-Being Scale (SWBS). SWBS, Ekşi and Kardaş. A 29-item instrument developed in Türkiye

The 2017 development study reports 29 items, five response categories and three dimensions, transcendence, harmony with nature and anomie. Overall internal consistency is reported as .886. This instrument is not a Turkish translation of the 20-item Paloutzian and Ellison SWBS. The abstract reports SRMR .50, while Table 6 gives .050 on page 84. MARSE records this unresolved source discrepancy rather than silently correcting it. Exploratory and confirmatory analyses use the same data. Independent validation, cross-group measurement invariance and clinical cut-offs are not established by this record.

doi.org
1983 · Assessment resource

Spiritual Well-Being Scale (SWBS). SWBS, Paloutzian and Ellison. The 20-item instrument and Turkish form

Twenty items use six response categories to assess religious and existential wellbeing. Each subscale has ten items. The religious domain includes a relationship with God. This is a different instrument from the 29-item Ekşi and Kardaş scale. The author hosts a Turkish form dated 2022. Availability of a translation does not establish Turkish psychometric validity or measurement invariance, neither of which is verified here. No-cost use requires proper citation and retention of the copyright byline. MARSE does not republish items or the scoring form. The two SWBS scores must not be assumed equivalent.
