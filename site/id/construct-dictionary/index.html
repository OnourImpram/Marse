Kamus Konstruk dan Konsep

Menjelaskan batas antarkonstruk yang berdekatan tanpa mengubah definisi menjadi diagnosis atau penilaian klinis.

Sumber pada lapisan ini telah diperiksa. Butir instrumen tidak dipublikasikan ulang.

Spiritual distress

Distress involving spiritual meaning, connection or conflict. It is not interchangeable with low spiritual well-being and is not a diagnosis.

Religious and spiritual struggless

Conflict or tension involving divine, interpersonal, moral, doubt or ultimate-meaning domains. Positive and negative consequences are both possible.

Religious trauma

A contested umbrella term. Distinguish traumatic events, adverse religious experiences, coercion, stigma and post-traumatic outcomes rather than assume a single syndrome.

Worldview and nonreligion

Religious, spiritual, secular, atheist, agnostic and other meaning systems are possible worldviews. None is treated as the default.

Spiritual bypassing

Use of spiritual frameworks in ways that may avoid unresolved psychological distress. It is culturally contingent and not a diagnostic label.

Cultural humility

An ongoing reflective stance toward power, assumptions and the person’s own meaning system, not a claim of mastering a culture.

Measurement invariance

Evidence that scores can be compared across groups or time under specified assumptions. Translation or good internal consistency alone does not establish invariance.

Adverse religious experiences

A descriptive category for coercion, stigma, abuse, exclusion or spiritually harmful experiences. Mechanism and outcome should be recorded separately.
